# RSS Dashboard 2.7.0

RSS Dashboard 2.7.0 brings your starred articles over from other feed readers,
makes your data safer and easier to find, and gives you more control over what
retention keeps. It also adds an image lightbox, a redesigned podcast episode
list, new grouping options, and a What's New summary after each update.

---

## Import your starred articles

- Import starred articles from a Google Reader-compatible `starred.json`
  export, confirmed with Inoreader and FreshRSS. Start it from the command
  palette, the Feed Manager, or **Settings → Import/Export**.
- Preview everything before it lands: articles are grouped by source feed, you
  pick exactly what to import, and entries that can't be imported are listed
  with a reason.
- Feeds you don't already follow are created for you in one folder of your
  choice (**Starred imports** by default).
- Labels from the export become tags, and you can adjust each article's tags in
  the preview. Any tags the import would add to your palette are shown first.
- Re-running the import updates what you already imported instead of
  duplicating it.
- Full article text is fetched only when you ask, using **Fetch now** in the
  reader.

See the [Import Starred Articles Guide](../starred-import-guide.md) for details.

## Safer storage

- Fixed Shard storage v2 losing read, starred, tag, saved, and playback state
  when a feed hadn't loaded or synced yet, and stopped that loss from spreading
  to other devices through sync.
- Shard storage v2 now cleans up state for articles that have been gone from
  their feed for 90 days, without touching feeds that haven't loaded.
- Deleting a feed now removes its shard file, including in the default hidden
  storage folder. Missing or corrupted shards are flagged in the sidebar and
  rebuilt automatically.
- Fixed reverting to Legacy JSON loading an empty dashboard, and fixed an
  unreadable settings file resetting your configuration to defaults.
- Backups now reliably write a data snapshot when the backup setting is on.
- **Settings → Storage** now shows where your data is actually stored, and
  points out a leftover `user-state.json` from an earlier setup.
- Export and import your feeds and your app settings separately with the new
  **Feed bundle** and **Settings bundle**.

## Storage modes are being retired

Legacy JSON and Shard storage v1 are now deprecated and will become read-only
in 3.0. The storage prompt explains what stops working and can be skipped for
one release at a time, but no longer permanently dismissed. If you are on
either mode, switch to Shard storage v2 from **Settings → Storage** before 3.0.

## Control what retention keeps

- Choose whether starred, saved, tagged, and unread articles are protected from
  automatic cleanup. Existing users keep starred and saved protected.
- Unread articles past a feed's auto-delete cutoff are now removed unless you
  protect them.
- Articles with no publish date now show "Unknown date" instead of "Invalid
  Date". Turn on **Use first-seen date for undated items** to sort and keep
  them by the date RSS Dashboard first saw them.

## Reading, listening, and organizing

- Tap an image in the reader to open it full size, with zoom and pan.
- Browse podcasts with a simpler episode list that loads more on demand, with
  tidier player controls on mobile.
- Group articles by **Date > Feed** or **Folder > Feed**.
- Move several selected feeds and folders at once with **Move selection to
  folder**.
- Delete a feed from the Edit feed window, or clear every feed and folder from
  the Feed Manager.
- Saved-note templates gain `{{saveDate}}`, `{{saveTime12}}`, `{{saveTime24}}`,
  and `{{firstSeen}}`.
- A short What's New summary appears once after an update, and can be reopened
  from **Settings → About**.
- AI Weekly joins the Discover catalog.

## Notable fixes

- Automatic refresh no longer causes repeated notifications, list flashes, or
  scroll jumps, and each feed's refresh indicator clears as soon as that feed
  finishes. The Stop button now appears on mobile.
- Starring an article no longer adds or removes a "Favorite" tag.
- Adding a Discover feed to **Root (no folder)** or to a newly created folder
  now puts it exactly there.
- Selecting several sidebar feeds now drags, deletes, and moves all of them.
- Fixed saved-note frontmatter breaking on titles that contain quotes.
- Fixed several problems with feeds loaded through the RSS2JSON fallback,
  including made-up dates and languages and articles overwriting each other's
  state.

## Removed

Nitter support and automatic X/Twitter feed conversion are removed following
Nitter's shutdown. Adding an X or Twitter URL now explains this and suggests an
RSS bridge instead.

## Compatibility and maintenance

RSS Dashboard now requires Obsidian 1.8.7 or later. This release also hardens
the release workflow and resolves the remaining Community Plugin scorecard
findings.

The full list of changes is in the [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md).
