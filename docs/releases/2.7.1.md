# RSS Dashboard 2.7.1

2.7.1 is a small follow-up to 2.7.0. If you are updating from 2.6.0, the [2.7.0 release notes](2.7.0.md) describe everything that is new in the 2.7 line.

## Fixes

- The **Import OPML/XML**, **Export OPML**, and **Import starred articles** buttons in Manage Feeds are readable again in Obsidian's light theme.

## Known issues

The known issues listed in the [2.7.0 release notes](2.7.0.md#known-issues) still apply. Two of them, a failed settings load that can let a later change overwrite your saved settings (#447) and podcast progress being replaced by an older resume point (#468), are fixed for a later release.
