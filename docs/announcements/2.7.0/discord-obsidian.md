# RSS Dashboard 2.7.0 — Obsidian Discord

Published: 2026-09-23
Platform: Obsidian Discord
Release: 2.7.0

---

**RSS Dashboard 2.7.0 is now available.**

Highlights in this release:

- Import starred articles from Inoreader, FreshRSS, and other supported Google Reader-compatible `starred.json` exports, with preview, tag import, and automatic feed creation.
- Open Reader images in a full-size lightbox with zoom and pan.
- Choose whether starred, saved, tagged, and unread articles are protected from automatic retention cleanup.
- Browse podcasts with a redesigned episode list and improved mobile controls.
- New **Date > Feed** and **Folder > Feed** grouping options.
- Starring and tagging are now independent; starring an article no longer automatically adds a `Favorite` tag.
- Significant storage, sync, backup, and recovery fixes.
- A new **What's New** summary now appears after major updates.

**Storage notice:** Legacy JSON and Shard storage v1 are now deprecated and will become read-only in RSS Dashboard 3.0. Users on either mode should migrate to **Shard storage v2** from **Settings → Storage** before upgrading to 3.0.

Available through the Obsidian Community Plugins updater.
