# RSS Dashboard 2.7.0

![RSS Dashboard logo](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/amatya-aditya/obsidian-rss-dashboard/master/assets/branding/logo.png)

Welcome to RSS Dashboard 2.7.0! Here’s a quick look at what’s new.

## Image lightbox

You can now click or tap any image in the reader to open a larger version with zoom and pan. Try it now by clicking the RSS Dashboard logo above!

## Starred article import

Bring your starred articles with you from Inoreader, FreshRSS, and other supported Google Reader-compatible exports.

Preview everything before importing, keep exported labels as tags, and automatically add feeds you don’t already follow. Imported articles start with the content included in the export; when you want the full article, just select **Fetch now** in the reader.

Find **Import starred articles** in the command palette, **Settings → Import/Export**, or **Manage Feeds** in the sidebar.

## Also new

- Choose whether starred, saved, tagged, and unread articles are protected from automatic cleanup.
- Browse podcasts with a simpler episode list and cleaner mobile controls.
- Organize articles with new **Date > Feed** and **Folder > Feed** grouping options.
- Starring and tagging are now independent—starring an article no longer adds a **Favorite** tag.

> **From the archives:** Google Reader users were combining stars and tags into personal workflows back in 2008. [This old Google Reader post](https://googlereader.blogspot.com/2008/11/better-cooking-through-reader-ing.html) is a fun example if you're looking for ideas on how the two can work together. Curious why RSS Dashboard made the change? Read the full decision in [ADR 0011](https://github.com/amatya-aditya/obsidian-rss-dashboard/blob/master/docs/adr/0011-decouple-starred-state-from-tags.md).

## Important: storage changes coming soon in 3.0

**Legacy JSON and Shard storage v1 are now deprecated** and will become read-only in RSS Dashboard 3.0.

If you still use either mode, switch to Shard storage v2 from **Settings → Storage** before upgrading to 3.0.

## Thanks for using RSS Dashboard

You can reopen this summary any time from **Settings → About**.
