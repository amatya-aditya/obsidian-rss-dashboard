# RSS Dashboard 2.7.0

![RSS Dashboard logo](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/amatya-aditya/obsidian-rss-dashboard/master/assets/branding/logo.png)

Welcome to 2.7. Here's what changed since your last update.

## Bring your starred articles with you

Import starred articles from Inoreader, FreshRSS, or another Google
Reader-compatible `starred.json` export. Preview them first, keep their labels
as tags, and re-import any time without duplicates. Find it in the command
palette as **Import starred articles**.

## Your data is safer

Shard storage v2 no longer loses read, starred, or tag state when a feed hasn't
synced yet, and deleted feeds no longer leave files behind.

**On Legacy JSON or Shard storage v1?** Those modes become read-only in 3.0.
Switch to Shard storage v2 from **Settings → Storage**.

## Also new

- Choose which articles retention protects: starred, saved, tagged, or unread.
  Unread articles past a feed's auto-delete cutoff are now removed unless
  protected.
- Tap a reader image to view it full size.
- A simpler podcast episode list.
- **Date > Feed** and **Folder > Feed** grouping.
- Starring no longer adds a "Favorite" tag.

Reopen this summary any time from **Settings → About**.
