# RSS Dashboard 2.8.0

![RSS Dashboard logo](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/amatya-aditya/obsidian-rss-dashboard/master/assets/branding/logo.png)

**[Join the Discord](https://discord.gg/9bu7V9BBbs) · [Visit GitHub](https://github.com/amatya-aditya/obsidian-rss-dashboard)**

RSS Dashboard 2.8.0 makes reading faster and gives you more control over how articles look and are saved. Read RSS content while the full article loads in the background, then switch to its fetched version in the Reader. Fetch or replace cover images, customize tag colors, and tailor saved-note templates for individual feeds. Navigate timestamped podcast chapters and preview sleep-timer progress. Startup is faster, article interactions respond sooner, and accessibility, media support, and sync reliability have improved.

## Faster reading and performance

- **Read while articles load.** Before today, articles only showed data that came through the RSS feed. The only way to see a live version of the article was to open a browser, forcing you to leave Obsidian. With 2.8, articles can be fetched directly inside the reader, with the ability to switch back to the RSS data version on demand. Your reading position will be saved independently for each version. Failed fetches are remembered rather than repeatedly attempted on every open, and you can retry when you're ready.
- **Much faster startup.** Redundant file reads, article copying, and repeated settings upgrades have been eliminated from key startup paths. In a test vault with 205 feeds, measured plugin load time fell from approximately 3.2–3.3 seconds to 1.5–1.8 seconds.
- **Instant article-state updates.** Marking articles read or unread now updates the list, sidebar unread count, and filter counts immediately, without waiting for the save to finish. Stars, tags, saved-note associations, and playback progress also use a more efficient persistence path. Users with many feeds should see a significant performance improvement.
- **More durable article state.** A new device-local journal records article-state changes immediately, while shared-state checkpointing happens in the background. Recent changes can survive an abrupt Obsidian close or a synced settings reload without being lost.

## Cover images

- **Fetch and restore article covers.** Before today, cover images were populated using only the data that was available in the RSS feed. With 2.8, you can now fetch an article's cover image directly from its webpage. This means you can now replace a missing cover (if the site provides one), replace the RSS-version's existing cover, or revert between the RSS version's cover and the Fetched version's cover.
- Covers can be fetched per-article with a new button inside the card, or batch fetch all covers with a new dashboard toolbar button alongside the filter button. This button can be hidden via settings.

## Tag customization

- **Tags you can style.** Tags have received a major overhaul. You can now customize the tag text color or select from a collection of predefined theme colors.
- Under **Settings → Tags**, choose a default background and text color, and every tag without its own color follows it. Pick from the **System**, **Vintage**, **Pastel** and **High Contrast** palettes or press **Randomize** to cycle through colors.
- The **Edit tag** dialog also includes the new color controls.

## Save articles your way

- **Flexible saved-note templates.** The article saving features received a significant upgrade. You can now edit templates independently, choose a global default, assign different templates to individual feeds, and configure custom folders and filename patterns. Filenames are handled safely to avoid collisions while preserving identified note paths.
- **Choose which content to save.** The new **Default save version** setting lets you choose between RSS content and fetched article content. Individual templates can override that choice, so different feeds can use different versions without changing your global preference.
- **More control over each save.** Dashboard save actions now offer **Save with default settings** and **Save to custom folder...**, matching the Reader. Choose the destination and filename pattern for an individual save.
- **More informative saved notes.** The new `{{contentSource}}` template variable records whether a note contains RSS content or fetched content. Saved notes also preserve HTML tables as Markdown tables and use the same cleaned fetched article body displayed in the readers.

The built-in default template includes `{{contentSource}}`. If you've already customized and saved your own default template, add `contentSource: "{{contentSource}}"` manually or reset the default template under **Settings → Article Saving** to include it. You may also opt in to always save a feed as the RSS or Fetched version as part of your template.

## Listening and media

- **Podcast chapter markers.** Episodes with timestamped chapters in their descriptions now show clickable markers on the seek bar. Hover or focus a marker to preview the chapter's title, start time, and length, or select it to jump directly to that chapter. Markers can be enabled or disabled under **Settings → Media → Podcast player**. Only episodes whose descriptions include chapter timestamps show markers.
- **Preview the sleep timer.** A second progress band shows how far playback is expected to advance before the sleep timer stops it. The preview accounts for playback speed and responds immediately when you seek or change speed. Both the chapter markers and sleep-timer preview are enabled by default and can be turned off in settings.
- **Expanded video playback.** Twitch VODs and live channels now play in the Reader and inline reader. YouTube videos embedded in feed content can play inline on demand, and saving a YouTube video item now produces a playable embed with a **Watch on YouTube** link.
- **Video Focus mode.** Focus mode now works with Twitch as well as YouTube, hiding the watch link and source line along with surrounding article details. Enable it under **Settings → Media → Video player** or toggle it using the command palette.

## Accessibility and dashboard usability

- **Better keyboard navigation and screen-reader support.** Dashboard and Reader controls are easier to reach and operate using the keyboard. Focus is handled more consistently when menus open, articles leave a filtered list, or settings tabs change. Screen readers receive clearer announcements for refresh activity and interface controls.
- **Respect for reduced motion.** RSS Dashboard now honors the operating system's reduce-motion setting, reducing decorative transitions and animations while keeping useful loading indicators visible.
- **Rename tags in one place.** The Tags settings tab now lets you rename a tag directly. The new name propagates to article tags, folder auto-tag rules, per-feed custom tags, and Auto tagging defaults so the tag continues to work throughout your setup.
- **Clearer keyboard shortcuts.** The shortcut reference groups actions by where they work, and dashboard controls identify their corresponding shortcuts. You can also return the article list to the start of the current view without losing your filters or search.
- **More consistent navigation.** Discover filters are easier to select, folder controls have larger touch targets, and search queries remain intact when the dashboard redraws, changes pages, refreshes, or switches views.

## Display and phones

- **Swipe between panels on mobile.** The new **Swipe between panels** setting under **Settings → General → Mobile navigation** lets you swipe right to open the sidebar, left to return to the article, and right in the Reader to go back to the dashboard. It replaces Obsidian's own swipe gestures inside this plugin's views and doesn't apply when the Reader opens in a sidebar.
- **Card press animation.** Turn the card press animation off under **Settings → Display → Card press animation**. When your system prefers reduced motion, card press animations stay off. Touch devices no longer show a second focus border, a changed cover background, or a stuck star preview after tapping.
- **Article title size.** An **Article title size** slider under **Settings → Display** scales titles in Card, List and Feed views from 50% to 200%.
- **Bluesky posts.** Posts opened in the Reader's fetched content show the post's text and images.
- **Reddit feeds in Discover.** Reddit feeds are removed from Discover ahead of Reddit's RSS retirement. Your existing subscriptions are unchanged.

## Notable fixes

- **Saved content matches the Reader.** Notes saved from fetched articles now use the same cleaned body displayed in the readers, avoiding repeated introductions, navigation elements, and duplicated lead media.
- **More reliable syncing.** Changes to feeds, folders, settings, and article state are less likely to overwrite unrelated changes arriving from another device. Shard storage v2 also handles incoming shard changes more reliably.
- **More reliable feed parsing.** Feeds that use namespace-prefixed RSS or Atom elements now import correctly, and relative article links resolve against the feed's declared website when appropriate.
- **Safer interactions.** Destructive settings actions request confirmation, mobile cover controls are easier to tap without accidentally opening an article, and several keyboard-focus and interface-state issues have been corrected.

## Known issues

If you store the metadata `data.json` in a vault folder using **Settings → Storage → Metadata data.json location**, an import can be lost if Obsidian closes before another change is saved. After importing, change a setting or mark an article as read before closing Obsidian. [GH Issue #474](https://github.com/amatya-aditya/obsidian-rss-dashboard/issues/474)
