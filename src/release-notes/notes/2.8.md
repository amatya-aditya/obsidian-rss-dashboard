# RSS Dashboard 2.8.0

![RSS Dashboard logo](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/amatya-aditya/obsidian-rss-dashboard/master/assets/branding/logo.png)

Welcome to RSS Dashboard 2.8.0! Here’s a quick look at what’s new.

## Faster reading and a more responsive dashboard

The Reader now shows available RSS content while the full article loads in the background. Switch between RSS and fetched content without losing your reading position in either version.

Startup is faster on large feed libraries. Article-state changes—such as marking an article read, starring it, tagging it, or updating playback progress—also take effect immediately instead of waiting for a lengthy save. Changes are recorded locally right away and saved in the background, helping protect them if Obsidian closes unexpectedly or a sync reload occurs.

## Save articles your way

Create and manage saved-note templates independently, choose a default, assign templates to individual feeds, and customize folders and filename patterns. You can also choose whether each template saves RSS content or fetched article content.

Dashboard save actions now offer **Save with default settings** and **Save to custom folder...**. Saved notes also preserve tables more accurately and can record which content version was saved with the `{{contentSource}}` template variable. Existing custom templates are not changed automatically; add the variable yourself or reset the default template under **Settings → Article Saving**.

## Podcast player upgrades

Podcast episodes with timestamped chapters in their descriptions now show clickable chapter markers on the seek bar. Hover or focus a marker to preview its title and timing, or select it to jump to that chapter.

A new sleep-timer preview shows how far playback is expected to advance before the timer stops it, accounting for playback speed. **Chapter markers only appear on episodes whose descriptions include chapter timestamps.** Both features can be toggled under **Settings → Media → Podcast player**.

## Better covers and more media support

Fetch an article’s cover image from its webpage, replace an existing cover, or restore the image supplied by its feed. Twitch VODs and live channels now play in the Reader and inline reader, YouTube videos embedded in feed content can play on demand, and saved YouTube items can include playable video embeds. YouTube also gains an optional video-only Focus mode.

## Also new

- More keyboard and screen-reader support across the dashboard, Reader, settings, and menus.
- RSS Dashboard now respects your operating system’s reduce-motion setting.
- Rename tags from the Tags settings tab, with the new name carried through article tags and automatic tagging rules.
- More reliable syncing of article state, feeds, folders, and settings across devices.
- Better handling of publisher markup, article images, feed formats, and saved article content.

## Important: storage changes coming in 3.0

**Legacy JSON and Shard storage v1 are deprecated and will become read-only in RSS Dashboard 3.0.** If you still use either mode, switch to Shard storage v2 from **Settings → Storage** before upgrading to 3.0.

## Thanks for using RSS Dashboard

You can reopen this summary any time from **Settings → About**.

Want to help shape RSS Dashboard? Join the community on [Discord](https://discord.gg/9bu7V9BBbs) to share feedback, ask questions, or contribute to the project. You’ll also find contribution and sponsorship links in **Settings → About**.

Happy reading!
