Fixture article whose page declares a hero image

This page declares an image in its og:image meta tag that differs from the first image in its body. A feed that supplies its own cover still wins on the card and in the RSS version, while the fetched version's hero must be the page's declared image rather than the body image or the feed cover.

The paragraphs run well past one hundred and twenty characters so Readability treats them as the article body. The fixture server serves this page and both images from the repository, so the scenario reproduces without any site outside it.
